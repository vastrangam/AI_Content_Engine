ℹ How it's wired

The whole engine in five steps.
  1. You upload a product photo + optional details.
  2. The image + the Humanized Engine prompt (baked into this tool) are sent to your chosen AI.
  3. The AI reads the photo (colour, fabric, work, occasion) — this is the "Gemini image research" step.
  4. It writes the content using the engine's rules: humanized (no product-noun stuffing), self-checked, on-brand, per the outputs you ticked.
  5. You get it back here — copy or download. Paste listings to Shopify, captions to Instagram, lyrics to Suno.

Free path: Gemini does both the image reading and the writing — ₹0. Paid path: swap in Claude (best prose) or OpenAI in Settings; same tool, better writing.

For maximum quality, the full 1,000-line humanized engine can be pasted as the system prompt — this tool ships with a strong condensed version built in.
