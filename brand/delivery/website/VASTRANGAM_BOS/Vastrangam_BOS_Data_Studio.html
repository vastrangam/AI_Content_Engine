Drop .xlsx workbooks here or click to choose them — several at once is fine

Each file is filed by what is inside it, not by what it is called. A workbook with “<Company> Sale” and “<Company> Return” sheets becomes the e-commerce report — two companies or ten, the columns follow the sheets.

    What this will not do. It will not guess a price that is not on the price list, or a stitching rate that is not in the rate master. A missing rate is posted as ₹0 and the design is named, because a guessed rate is a wrong payment to a real person.

    Where the numbers come from. Sets are counted by pooling every karigar's pieces for a design first and then taking the smallest member pool; surplus pieces are reported one by one under their own names; cost is per raw piece, so a piece that never became part of a set is still paid for.
