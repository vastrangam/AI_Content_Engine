Medhava at a glance

What it is
A unified ERP: __NMOD__ modules and __NAPP__ apps sharing one data core and one event bus.
Shared data core
Item/SKU · Party · Stock · Ledger & Voucher · Order — every module reads and writes these.
Key difference
Not a suite of integrated apps. One application, so there is no sync step and no duplicate master data.
Compliance
Double-entry accounting with CGST/SGST/IGST, TDS, TCS, ITC on accepted goods, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.
Channels
Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nykaa, JioMart, plus Shopify and WooCommerce.
Security
PostgreSQL row-level security per company; integrations use revocable scoped API keys in an AES-256-GCM vault — never account passwords.
Deployment
Hosted multi-tenant cloud, or single-file offline apps that run by double-clicking with no install.
The problem

Your business runs on eleven tools that don't talk

Accounting in one place, stock in a spreadsheet, marketplace orders in seven seller panels, karigar wages in a notebook. Every month-end becomes an archaeology project.

⛓️‍💥

Numbers that disagree

Stock says 40, the marketplace says 12, the books say something else again. Nobody knows which is right, so everyone guesses.

⏳

Work entered three times

The same invoice keyed into the seller panel, the spreadsheet and the accounting package. Three chances to be wrong.

🕳️

Money quietly leaking

Over-billed purchases, unclaimed marketplace shortfalls, RTO losses — invisible until the year is over.

How it works

One data core. One action cascades everywhere.

Every module reads and writes the same five entities. That is the physical reason a single goods receipt can touch stock, books, quality and sourcing at the same instant.

Accepted — not ordered — is what counts

You order 100 metres. 100 arrive. Quality accepts 96. Most systems increase stock by 100 and claim tax credit on 100. Medhava increases stock by 96, claims input tax credit on 96, raises a debit note for the 4 rejected, and lowers that mill's accept rate — automatically.

Nothing derived is ever stored

Outstanding, risk, performance, aging, profit-per-design — all recomputed on read. They cannot drift out of step with the documents underneath them.
