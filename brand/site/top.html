What Medhava is

One application. Not eleven that talk to each other.

Every other suite sells you separate products and a sync job between them. Medhava is a single application over one set of records — so there is nothing to synchronise, and nothing to reconcile.

1

Enter it once

A goods receipt, a counter sale, a marketplace order — recorded in one place. Stock, the ledger, the quality record and the supplier scorecard all update in the same instant, because they are reading the same record.

2

Numbers that cannot disagree

One stock figure per SKU. One ledger. One order record. Not a copy per channel that somebody reconciles on Saturday. When the website says four are left, four are left.

3

Built for how India actually runs

GST with input credit on accepted goods, TDS and TCS, seven marketplaces, __PR_SHORT__ — and apps that keep working when the internet does not.

How it works

One data core. One action cascades everywhere.

Every module reads and writes the same five entities. That is the physical reason a single goods receipt can touch stock, books, quality and sourcing at the same instant.

Accepted — not ordered — is what counts

You order 100 metres. 100 arrive. Quality accepts 96. Most systems increase stock by 100 and claim tax credit on 100. Medhava increases stock by 96, claims input tax credit on 96, raises a debit note for the 4 rejected, and lowers that __SUPPLIER__'s accept rate — automatically.

Nothing derived is ever stored

Outstanding, risk, performance, ageing, profit per product — all recomputed on read. They cannot drift out of step with the documents underneath them.
