# Medhava · HR / Payroll

> Headcount, attendance & pro-rated payroll — salaries from days present.

**Real, running software** — open `app.html` by double-clicking (any modern browser, works offline). Your data is saved in the browser (localStorage) and survives refreshes.

## Screens
- **Dashboard**
- **Team**
- **Payroll**

## What works
A people register. Monthly pay is pro-rated on days present out of working days, so marking attendance re-computes payroll instantly. Headcount, average attendance and department cost are all derived.

- Seeded with realistic demo data on first open.

- Export/import a JSON backup; reload demo data; wipe — all under **Backup & Health**.
